# Play this first — 2026-08-01

Eight things shipped to the 3D game in one day and none have been played. This is the order to
try them in, on a **phone, in landscape**, which is the target device.

Live: https://stephenuffugus.github.io/abduct_a_chameleon/abduct-3d.html
(hard-refresh, or use the portal card which is cache-busted to `?v=20260801h`)

## 1. Open the paint studio (🎨)
It used to be a **softlock**: the panel rendered 703px tall inside a 430px screen, so the ✕ was
123px above the top of the display and DONE was 76px below the bottom. There was no way out but a
reload. Check both exits are there and that you can leave.

## 2. Tap MATCH GROUND
It should read **100%**, not 65%. A perfect paint used to score 65% because the colour maths
compared two different colour spaces, which also meant camo could never pass the hunter's 0.70 and
0.75 thresholds — **the reward for painting well did not exist.** Now stand still and watch HIDDEN
climb to 100%, and wait for **⭐ EARNING** to appear when the training UFO looks at you and sees
nothing.

## 3. Walk next to a crate or a boulder
The studio should show **two** things you are hiding against, and a **SPLIT · TOP HALF** button.
One colour caps out around 0.65 there; the split gets you to 1.00. **This is the question: does
that tax feel fair, or annoying?**

## 4. Press JUMP (or Space)
Get on top of a crate, a boulder, a bench, a picnic table, the slide deck. The studio should say
**"BOULDER · you are standing on it"** — the thing under your feet becomes what you paint. **Is the
jump height right?**

## 5. Press H, or tap HUNT
You have never flown the UFO. Six painted bodies are hiding with a real spread of skill — two are
nearly invisible, two are gettable. Beam them. Clear all six and it reseeds. **Is spotting them
fun, or frustrating?**

## 6. Look around a map
Spray tags, stencils, scorch marks, chalk, litter, a playground, a park with a fountain, stone
landmarks. Try **downtown** or **ruins** from the 🗺 picker — they have the most walls and read
best. **Is it too busy? Not busy enough?**

## 7. The hunter's two minutes
Only visible with a second player, when you are the seeker. It is now the **Abduction Files** —
880 pages, and the ones you recover are remembered.

## What I most need to hear
- Does the two-tone tax feel fair?
- Is the jump height right?
- Is the detail too busy on a phone screen?
- Does the game run smoothly on your Pixel? (340 marks are instanced to 14 draw calls, but your
  phone is the only real test.)
