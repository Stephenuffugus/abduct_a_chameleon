HOW TO PLAY

Abduct a Chameleon 3D

The goal

Alien ships are hunting the ground for anything that does not belong. If you are a chameleon, paint your body to match what you are standing on and survive until the timer runs out. If you are a hunter, fly the ship, find the one shape whose colour is wrong, and beam it up.

How it plays

Hiders get a head start to choose a spot and paint themselves while the scanners are still warming up. Colour is the whole game: the closer your paint is to the ground beneath you, the longer a hunter's eye slides straight past. Movement is what gives you away, so pick your spot and settle.

While you wait

A hunter's scanners are dark for the first two minutes, and a chameleon who gets beamed up is out for the rest of the round. Both get the same thing to do: CALIBRATION. You are shown a still frame of the valley with three painted bodies hidden somewhere in it — tap the one whose colour is closest to the ground it is standing on. Get it right and you recover a page of the Abduction Files, which is the only way to read them. Three a wait, and no more.

Getting into the same game

The panel on the left is the lobby. It shows your room, how many people are actually in it, and whether they picked HIDE or SEEK. If it says only you are here, you are not in the same room as the other person — tap 📷 QR, hold their phone's code in the frame, and you will land in theirs. They can also just point their camera at your code, or you can type it in.

Nothing starts on its own. Somebody presses START ROUND, everybody sees a three-second countdown, and then the round begins. Until then it is free play: everyone is a chameleon, nobody can be caught, and the paint studio is open.

The controls, on a phone

The left half of the screen moves you and does nothing else. Every button is on the right, so you never have to stop walking to press one.

  • Left half of the screen moves you
  • Right half looks around
  • JUMP sits by your right thumb — get on top of a crate and paint yourself up there
  • The paint button (🎨) opens the paint studio, where you colour your own body
  • The view button (👁) switches between your own eyes and a look from above
  • The pose button (🕺) changes your shape, because a shape can hide as well as a colour
  • The taunt button (📣) pulls a hunter somewhere you are not
  • The decoy button (👥) leaves behind something that looks like you
  • A hunter gets BEAM, SCAN, LAND and ▲ ▼ for altitude

Hunting: the three tools

  • BEAM — hold it over a chameleon for two seconds and they are taken. It drains the ship's cell, and it burns out if you empty it.
  • SCAN — sweep the place you are over. Over a building it sweeps that building; over open ground it sweeps a patch. Three seconds later it tells you one thing only: how many chameleons are in there — never where. A swept room stays marked on your view for half a minute, red for occupied, blue for clear. This is how you narrow a map down: sweep from the air, find the room, then land and search it on foot. The chameleons inside are told their room is being swept, so they get a few seconds to decide whether to run for somewhere you have already cleared — and running is what gets people caught.
  • LAND — set the ship down and hunt on foot. A roof is a roof: no altitude ever lets you see under one. On the ground you lose the view from above and the beam, and you carry a stun gun instead — nine metres, inside a narrow cone you have to be aiming, in clear sight. A stunned chameleon cannot move for twenty seconds: that is your window to get back to the ship, fly over and beam them up, and a stunned one can be taken straight through a roof. Everyone you have already caught is sitting in that parked ship while you do it.

Fighting back

You cannot hurt an alien, but you are not helpless either.

  • STICK — the sticky tongue. It pins an alien where it stands for eight seconds, which is not enough to beat them, only enough to get away and hide again. Long cooldown, and firing it gives your position away.
  • JAM — drop a scanner jammer. Any sweep of the block it sits in comes back occupied, whether anybody is in there or not. Plant one across the valley, hide somewhere else, and watch a hunter land and search six empty rooms. An alien on foot who walks into it will break it — and then they know they were played.
  • FREE — get your friends out. See below.

Nobody can be stunned twice in a row: once an effect wears off you are immune for a while, so nothing ever turns into two players locking each other in place forever.

Getting caught is not the end

A caught chameleon is carried around inside the ship. There is exactly one moment they can be reached: when that alien lands to search a building and leaves the saucer sitting on the ground. Walk up to it and hold FREE for three and a half seconds.

That is the whole bargain for the hunter — see under the roofs, or guard what you have caught. They cannot do both. And for you it is the most dangerous thing in the game: you have to leave cover, stand still in the open next to an alien's ship, and hope they are still deep inside a building. Freeing somebody is worth more than surviving a round on your own.

The controls, on a keyboard

WASD moves, the mouse looks, Space jumps, E paints, V changes view, Q changes pose, T taunts, F drops a decoy, C fires the sticky tongue, X plants a jammer, G sweeps the area, L lands the ship or takes off again, R (held) frees a teammate from a parked ship, and Escape backs out of the paint studio — or opens Settings when nothing else is open.

If the looking feels wrong

Settings has a look sensitivity slider, an invert look Y switch and a master volume dial. They are yours alone — nobody else in the match is affected — and they are remembered on this device.
